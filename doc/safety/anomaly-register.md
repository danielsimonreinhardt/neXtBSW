## 1. Scope

### 1.1 What counts as a safety anomaly

2-5.4.3 asks that anomalies found during the safety lifecycle are communicated, analysed and either resolved or
closed with a rationale that someone has reviewed, and that an anomaly that is not closed is brought to the person
responsible for functional safety. neXtBSW has no issue tracker for this; its findings live in the finding tables
of the safety documents and in the tasks of `doc/todo.yaml`. The register takes as a **safety anomaly** every row
of these tables:

(rows are appended, never renumbered or deleted).

### 1.2 What is deliberately not in the register

| Not registered | Why | Where it is managed |
|---|---|---|
| Questions to the user (Q1 … of `iso26262-gap.md`, Q-CM, U and Q-S of `rtos-comparison.md`, TN1 … TN5) | they ask for a decision, they do not report a defect | decision board; the answer goes into `doc/todo.yaml` |

| Warnings of `tools/trace` (`traceability.md`, "Open trace findings") | generated and re-checked in every CI run (`safety-trace`); each points to an open task (DOC-19, SSR under SYS-30 … SYS-33) | `tools/trace`, `traceability.md` |

A finding table added to a safety document later (for example by SYS-61, SYS-71 or SYS-92 of the same wave) is
added to `SOURCES` of the consistency test by the coordinator at the merge; from then on its rows must be here.

## 2. Rules

### 2.1 Life cycle

| Status | Meaning | Required in the row |
|---|---|---|
| `open` | nothing done yet | owner = an open task of `doc/todo.yaml`, or a role; a row with a role only is escalated (section 4) |
| `in progress` | a task changed part of it, the rest has an open task | as `open`, plus "Closed by" the tasks that did the part done |
| `review` | fixed and host-verified, but a board proof, the acceptance of the closing task or a follow-up edit is outstanding | "Closed by" and the rationale naming what is outstanding |
| `closed` | fixed, with evidence, nothing outstanding for stage 1 | "Closed by", evidence, reviewer other than the author; none of the closing tasks may still be `open` in `doc/todo.yaml` |
| `accepted` | deliberately not fixed, with a rationale (usually an assumption of use or a decision that stage 1 does not need it) | the **user's decision** as reviewer: only the safety manager accepts a residual (safety plan section 2) |

A row never goes back to "unknown": if a closure turns out wrong, the row returns to `open` and the rationale says
why (the history is in git).

### 2.2 Who does what

| Step | Who | How |
|---|---|---|
| Raise | the author of the analysis or review (agent) | a row in the finding table of the document the agent works in, with ID, finding, why it matters and a proposed owner |
| Register | the coordinator at the merge (or the agent, if `doc/safety/anomaly-register.md` is in its paths) | a new `AN-nnn` row; the test fails until it exists |
| Analyse and resolve | the task that owns it | the change, its tests and a changelog fragment that names the finding ID |
| Close | the coordinator at the merge of the closing task | status, "Closed by", rationale, evidence; "Reviewer" = `coordinator (merge <task>)` for a closure checked by re-running the tests at the merge |
| Accept a residual | the user as safety manager on the decision board | "Reviewer" = `user (<decision ID>)` |
| Escalate | the coordinator | a row in section 4 and an item on the decision board; the safety manager assigns a task or decides |
| Confirm | the confirmation reviewer once named (SYS-63, iso-q1) | not yet possible; see section 2.3 |

### 2.3 What "reviewer" means here

At ASIL B the closure of an anomaly needs a reviewed rationale, not an independent one. Two reviews are recorded:

- `coordinator (merge <task>)` — the coordinator re-ran the tests and the target build of the closing task before the
  merge and checked that the change answers the finding. The coordinator did not write the change, so this is a
  review by someone other than the author; it is still the commissioning side (safety plan section 2).
- `user (<decision ID>)` — the user accepted the result or the residual on the decision board.

Neither is a confirmation review at independence I1. When SYS-63 names a reviewer, its protocol template gets a line
per closed anomaly of the reviewed work product, and the register records the protocol ID in the reviewer column.

### 2.4 Link to the release

A release that claims ASIL B capability (a **safety release**, `cm-plan.md` section 4.2) needs, in addition to the
preconditions R1 … R6 of the safety plan: no row with status `open` or `in progress` whose source is a stage-1
mechanism without either an owner task planned for a later release or an acceptance by the user. **[Proposal]** for
the next issue of the safety plan (P2-03 objective); not binding before the safety manager accepts it.

## 6. Document register

8-10.4.5 asks that every document of the safety lifecycle carries its formal elements — title, author, approver,
a unique identification and version, change history and status — and that the current version can be found. The
safety documents carry title, issue and a history in prose, but no author and no approver and not always a status.
Until DOC-21 carries them as document fields, this table is their home. Columns:

- **Author** — the task(s) whose agent wrote the document on the user's behalf (the agent's session and branch are
  recorded in `doc/todo.yaml` and in the changelog entry of the task).
- **Approver** — the user as safety manager with the decision that accepted the current issue, `pending (<item>)`
  when the acceptance waits for a decision board item, `—` for a generated document.
- **Issue** — the issue number and date of the document's own header.
- **Status** — `draft` (written, no acceptance requested yet), `in review` (merged, acceptance on the decision board
  pending), `accepted` (the user accepted the issue named), `generated` (derived by a tool, approved through its
  sources).
- **State line** — whether the header carries the line of `cm-plan.md` section 6 (`Baseline:` or `Working state:`).
- **Change history** — where the history is kept.

  accepted rows are updated.
