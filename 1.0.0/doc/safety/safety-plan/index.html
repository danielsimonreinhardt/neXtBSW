    What it is not. Not a safety case (SYS-64), not a safety manual (DOC-19), not a statement that neXtBSW complies. The plan says what is done, by whom and with which result; the evidence is in the work products it names.

    1. Scope and claim¶

    1.1 The element¶

    neXtBSW as described in concept.md section 1: the base software (MCU drivers, safety mechanisms of the base software, RTE, generators) as a software component SEooC (10-9.2.4) with ASIL B capability (10-9.1). Stage 1 argues ASIL B with the mechanisms of the base software only (concept 1.4); the device drivers' own mechanisms are stage 2 and not covered by this issue. The item, its hazard analysis, its safety goals, its hardware and the application software are outside the element (concept 1.2, AoU-01, AoU-09, AoU-17).

    1.2 The claim¶

    neXtBSW is developed following the methods of ISO 26262-6 at ASIL B, without the management part.

    Decided by the user on 2026-09-22 (decision board, iso-q2). What it means:

    • Parts 6, 8 and 9 are applied at ASIL B for the element, with the tailoring of section 3. Where a method is not applied the rationale is given in the work product (6-4.3 reading).
    • Part 2 is applied as far as a single-person open-source project can apply it: a safety plan (this document), roles, tailoring, safety case, confirmation measures and a release procedure. What cannot be provided is stated, not assumed:
    • no quality management system in the sense of 2-5.4.5;
    • no evidence of competence of the persons in the lifecycle in the sense of 2-5.4.4 — most work is written by agents on the user's behalf and reviewed by the coordinator and the user;
    • no independent confirmation review yet (2-6.4.9, 2-6.4.10): by the decision of the safety responsible of 2026-09-30 (AN-093, tailoring T-11) the user performs the confirmation measures of section 5 himself for now. That is a confirmation without independence: its records find and close findings, but no development state gets an ASIL statement from them, and no release may claim ASIL B capability until an independent person has reviewed it.
    • The integrator who uses neXtBSW in a safety-related item takes over these gaps under his own management of functional safety; the safety manual (DOC-19) states them as assumptions of use (gap analysis proposal C8).

    3. Tailoring of the lifecycle for the SEooC¶

    2-6.4.5.1 requires every tailoring to be defined in the safety plan with a rationale; 2-6.4.5.7 says for an SEooC that the requirements are derived from assumptions on intended use and context, including the external interfaces, and that the assumptions are validated at integration. The tailoring below applies these two clauses. A tailoring does not remove the objective: where an activity moves to the integrator, the safety manual (DOC-19) carries the assumption that makes the hand-over explicit.

    T-01 … T-10 are tailorings of the lifecycle. T-11 is not a tailoring but a deviation from a requirement that applies at ASIL B (the independence of the confirmation measures); it stands in the same table because every activity and every document names it by this ID, and it says what lifts it.

    R and P are one list (issue 2, CR-01-1 F-07). cm-plan.md section 5.1 states the same preconditions as P1 … P9, with how each is checked; a safety release needs all of them. The mapping:

    cm-plan Safety plan Note
    P1 R1 candidate complete on dev, VERSION and changelog section
    P2 R2 green CI run on the tagged tree, report bound and archived
    P3 R1 repository integrity before the tag
    P4 R3 state lines Baseline: vX.Y.Z
    P5 R4 qualification reports
    P6 R5 confirmation reviews at I1 and inspections closed
    P7 R1 the user's approval of merge and tag
    P8 — export of a release line (tools/distcheck); a configuration-management precondition without a safety counterpart
    P9 R6 safety case and safety manual of the release — binding through R6 since issue 1 of this plan

    A tag that does not meet R1–R6 is a development release (the term of cm-plan.md 4.2 and of the tag message): it may be tagged for reproducibility (SYS-68) but carries no safety claim, and its tag message says so. As long as no independent confirmation reviewer is named (R5, T-11), every tag is a development release. Releases of the open-source branch and of licensed branches (PLAT-46) follow the same rule per branch.

    7. Maintaining the plan¶

    • Owner: the safety manager. Kept up to date by: the coordinator.
    • Updates of the current issue: when a task named in section 4 or 5 changes its status to done, the coordinator sets the row's status in the same merge; when a new safety task is created, the coordinator adds it to the row it serves; when a proposal of section 8 becomes a task, the rows name the task instead of the proposal. Each update gets a line in the change history (section 12).
    • New issue (rule sharpened in issue 2 after CR-01-1 F-08): the plan gets a new issue when its argument changes — the claim, a role, a tailoring or deviation of section 3, a confirmation measure, a release precondition — and then CM-01 is repeated for the change (delta review, reviews/README.md section 4). Issue 1 missed this twice: the deviation T-11 came as an update, and the release v0.2.0 carried the plan unchecked as issue 1.
    • At a release: the plan is re-checked against the candidate and its state line becomes Baseline: vX.Y.Z (R3, cm-plan.md P4); if the plan was changed since its last confirmation review, the release is a development release or CM-01 is repeated first. Between releases the state line is Working state: dev at <commit> (after vX.Y.Z) (cm-plan.md 6.1).
    • Checked by: doc/safety/test/test_safety_plan.py. The test fails when a named task disappears from doc/todo.yaml, when a task is done although its work product is still missing, when a done row names a missing work product, or when a gap row of parts 2, 6, 8, 9 is no longer served; since issue 2 also when the plan lags behind: a row still names a proposal whose task is done (or a carried proposal at all), a row is planned although one of its tasks has started and its work products exist, a measure of section 5 is prepared although a record with a verdict exists (or performed/closed without one), or the mapping R ↔ P of section 6 misses a precondition of cm-plan.md 5.1. A red test is the signal to update this plan, not to relax the test.
    • Change impact: TOOL-57 added the optional field safety_impact to the tasks; until it is filled for the tasks that change the safety part (SYS-122, N9), the coordinator decides per merge whether a change affects a work product of this plan (8-8.4.3).
