    What this document is. Two registers the gap analysis iso26262-gap.md (not published, available on request) found missing (section 6 no. 24; rows 2-03 and 8-24) and the safety plan safety-plan.md plans as activities P2-03 (management of safety anomalies, 2-5.4.3) and P8-07 (documentation management, 8-10):

    1. the safety anomaly register (sections 2–4): every finding that the safety analyses, reviews and work products of neXtBSW have raised, in one table, with owner, status, the task that closed it, the closure rationale, the evidence and who checked the closure; open anomalies that no task carries are escalated to the safety manager;

    The consistency test test/test_anomaly_register.py extracts every finding from the finding tables of the safety documents and fails when one is not registered, and it checks each row against doc/todo.yaml.

    Licence rule. The standard is cited by part and clause only (for example "2-5.4.3" = ISO 26262-2:2018, clause 5.4.3). No text, table or figure of the standard is reproduced; requirements are restated in our own words.

    What it is not. Not the task list (doc/todo.yaml stays the place where work is planned; the register points to it), not the decision board (questions to the user stay there), and not a confirmation review: the column "Reviewer" records a verification by the coordinator or an acceptance by the user, both author side (safety plan section 2). The independent confirmation of closures is part of SYS-63.

    1. Scope¶

    1.1 What counts as a safety anomaly¶

    2-5.4.3 asks that anomalies found during the safety lifecycle are communicated, analysed and either resolved or closed with a rationale that someone has reviewed, and that an anomaly that is not closed is brought to the person responsible for functional safety. neXtBSW has no issue tracker for this; its findings live in the finding tables of the safety documents and in the tasks of doc/todo.yaml. The register takes as a safety anomaly every row of these tables:

    2. Rules¶

    2.1 Life cycle¶

    Status Meaning Required in the row
    open nothing done yet owner = an open task of doc/todo.yaml, or a role; a row with a role only is escalated (section 4)
    in progress a task changed part of it, the rest has an open task as open, plus "Closed by" the tasks that did the part done
    review fixed and host-verified, but a board proof, the acceptance of the closing task or a follow-up edit is outstanding "Closed by" and the rationale naming what is outstanding
    closed fixed, with evidence, nothing outstanding for stage 1 "Closed by", evidence, reviewer other than the author; none of the closing tasks may still be open in doc/todo.yaml
    accepted deliberately not fixed, with a rationale (usually an assumption of use or a decision that stage 1 does not need it) the user's decision as reviewer: only the safety manager accepts a residual (safety plan section 2)

    A row never goes back to "unknown": if a closure turns out wrong, the row returns to open and the rationale says why (the history is in git).

    2.2 Who does what¶

    Step Who How
    Raise the author of the analysis or review (agent) a row in the finding table of the document the agent works in, with ID, finding, why it matters and a proposed owner
    Register the coordinator at the merge (or the agent, if doc/safety/anomaly-register.md is in its paths) a new AN-nnn row; the test fails until it exists
    Analyse and resolve the task that owns it the change, its tests and a changelog fragment that names the finding ID
    Close the coordinator at the merge of the closing task status, "Closed by", rationale, evidence; "Reviewer" = coordinator (merge <task>) for a closure checked by re-running the tests at the merge
    Accept a residual the user as safety manager on the decision board "Reviewer" = user (<decision ID>)
    Escalate the coordinator a row in section 4 and an item on the decision board; the safety manager assigns a task or decides
    Confirm the confirmation reviewer once named (SYS-63, iso-q1) not yet possible; see section 2.3

    2.3 What "reviewer" means here¶

    At ASIL B the closure of an anomaly needs a reviewed rationale, not an independent one. Two reviews are recorded:

    • coordinator (merge <task>) — the coordinator re-ran the tests and the target build of the closing task before the merge and checked that the change answers the finding. The coordinator did not write the change, so this is a review by someone other than the author; it is still the commissioning side (safety plan section 2).
    • user (<decision ID>) — the user accepted the result or the residual on the decision board.

    Neither is a confirmation review at independence I1. When SYS-63 names a reviewer, its protocol template gets a line per closed anomaly of the reviewed work product, and the register records the protocol ID in the reviewer column.

    2.4 Link to the release¶

    A release that claims ASIL B capability (a safety release, cm-plan.md section 4.2) needs, in addition to the preconditions R1 … R6 of the safety plan: no row with status open or in progress whose source is a stage-1 mechanism without either an owner task planned for a later release or an acceptance by the user. [Proposal] for the next issue of the safety plan (P2-03 objective); not binding before the safety manager accepts it.
